# ADR-025.2 Phase E — Configuration, clients, APIs, and proofs

## Status

Implemented and qualified on 2026-08-04.

## Governed project authoring

The authenticated-map project schema now exposes all five authorization modes.
Governed collections bind a stable `authorizationPolicy`; the same public
blueprint can define:

- the stable administrator authority and threshold;
- revision-1 organizations, actors, roles, Ed25519 key epochs, and key proofs
  of possession;
- active direct-role and approval-policy revisions;
- the frozen governed evidence, genesis, pending-index, expiry, query, and
  crypto-work limits; and
- an optional, explicitly non-consensus post-genesis onboarding plan.

Resolution constructs the exact `GovernedGenesisV1` contract and rejects an
invalid status, revision, key proof, authority threshold, reference closure,
record bound, policy shape, or collection/policy kind. Basic-only projects
remain source compatible and reject stray governance configuration.

Rendering emits the canonical governed genesis, authority, limits, individual
organization/actor/policy records, identity and capability metadata, and a
public authorization guide under `config/authenticated-map/` and `docs/`.
Private actor and member keys are never rendered. The offline
`role key-proof-signature` command emits the exact raw 64-byte public genesis
proof expected by the blueprint while reading a private seed only from the
caller-selected file.

Doctor has a dedicated `authenticated-map-governed-readiness` check. Every
governed collection either resolves to an active genesis policy or fails with
`GOVERNED_COLLECTION_NOT_BOOTSTRAPPED` and the exact collection/policy pair.
An onboarding entry is reported as planned but does not make the collection
writable or weaken fail-closed startup.

## Client and offline command authoring

`AuthenticatedMapAuthoring` builds canonical complete actions, direct-role
external-signing requests, approval payload statements/references, and final
governed commands. Signature completion verifies the returned Ed25519
signature against the claimed key before constructing evidence.

`StdlibAppChainClient` submits governed map execution, signed approval
statements, actor governance, and policy governance on their versioned topics.
It also performs exact direct- and approval-consumption queries and rejects a
response whose retained subject differs from the requested actor,
authorization ID, or proposal.

The no-private-key `appchain authenticated-map` CLI exposes:

- `action` and `action-commitment`;
- `direct-preimage` and signature-verifying `direct-complete`;
- `approval-payload` and `approval-reference`; and
- final governed `command` assembly.

The distribution launcher and help surface route these commands. Actor keys
remain in an external wallet, HSM, or caller-owned offline signer.

## Bounded domain API

The first-party stdlib bundle manifests
`com.bloxbean.cardano.yano.appchain.stdlib` as a read-only domain API provider.
It composes the existing role-workflow routes and adds root-fixed routes for:

- authenticated-map metadata, exact entries, and receipts;
- direct and approval consumption;
- direct policies and administrator authorities, including historical
  revisions and current pointers;
- actor/policy governance records and component command results; and
- bounded pending approval, actor-governance, and policy-governance pages.

Exact-record responses carry the precise composite physical key and canonical
leaf value. Metadata pairs the genesis marker key with its actual 32-byte
genesis-ID leaf, not the larger capabilities query payload. Current-record
resolution rejects mixed roots and pointer/revision disagreement.

Pending pages are deterministic projections, not leaves. Their responses use
`sourceIndexProofKey`, canonical `queryValue`, and
`DERIVED_FROM_PENDING_INDEX`; they never mislabel page bytes as a value at the
source index key. JSON remains a presentation surface over canonical records.

## Composite proof verification

`AuthenticatedMapProofBundle` supports bounded `BASIC`, `DIRECT_ROLE`,
`APPROVAL`, and `ADMINISTRATOR_GOVERNANCE` assemblies. Every fact must match
the same trusted chain, profile, genesis, height, and root. The verifier
recomputes the frozen composite physical key for every map, actor, policy,
proposal, authority, governance, pointer, receipt, and consumption fact rather
than trusting a caller-supplied label.

Semantic verification additionally binds:

- an applied receipt and exact result to the displayed entry/tombstone;
- the receipt, action commitment, message, execution height, and one-time
  consumption record;
- direct statement/signature digests, coverage, lifetime, current policy and
  actor pointers, role, organization, and active key;
- approval payload/genesis/action, frozen policy digest, proposer eligibility,
  clause thresholds and distinctness, retained actor/organization revisions,
  and every accepted decision signature; and
- activated governance results, mutation and authority revisions, threshold,
  retained administrator votes, claimed keys, signatures, and namespaces.

`verify(...)` requires an independently trusted exact root;
`verifyCertified(...)` verifies each fact under caller-pinned finality
membership. A later-root proof establishes retained immutable state, not that
a pointer was current at an earlier height; callers use the decision/execution
root for that stronger claim.

## Review corrections

The Phase E review caught and corrected three proof-boundary issues before
qualification:

1. fact names alone did not prevent a valid record-shaped value from being
   substituted from another composite namespace;
2. a basic entry proof was not semantically tied to a result in its receipt;
3. metadata and pending projections paired proof keys with bytes that were not
   the corresponding leaf values.

Adversarial tests now retain independently valid unrelated leaves and confirm
that substitution is rejected.
